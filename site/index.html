How this maps to production

In production, replace the browser simulation with real agent endpoints, MCP/tool gateways, identity context, OTEL traces, versioned eval packs, deterministic validators, business telemetry and an LLM-as-judge service. Domain business eval definitions become versioned configuration that the central runner resolves to an evaluator and applies during release gating.
